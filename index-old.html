About Me

An avid roboticist, tinkerer, and enjoyer of learning new things. My research interests include medical and surgical robotics, soft robotics and sensing, controls, human-computer interaction (HCI), prosthetics, and programming language theory.

I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at Tufts University in Medford, Massachusetts. I then completed the Master's in Research (MRes) in Medical Robotics in The Hamlyn Centre at Imperial College London.

Between September 2022 and November 2023, I was a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Boston University. Since November 2023, I am a PhD student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Leeds.
